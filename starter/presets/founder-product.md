# Preset — founder & product

For founders, product managers and people building a product, a service or a small business. Used once by init-library; later by tune-up.

## Words
- Work item: **project** (a product, a feature area, a launch, a bet). `kind: project`

## Areas
- `ideas/` — raw ideas and opportunities, before any commitment
- `projects/` — what is actually being built or run
- `customers/` — one item per customer segment or important account, holding conversations and evidence
- `business/` — pricing, finances in outline, hiring, legal and admin threads, fundraising
- `decisions/` — optional: decisions big enough to deserve their own page

## Work item sections
Overview (what it is, for whom) · Problem (whose, how they cope today, how you know) · Hypothesis (we believe … for … will result in … we will know when …) · Evidence so far (for and against, with sources) · Plan (now / next / later) · Measures (the one or two numbers that show it is working) · Risks and unknowns (the riskiest assumption first) · Decisions (date · decision · why) · Current state · Open questions · Files · Related

## Routines

### customer-conversation
One meeting note per conversation, filed under the customer item:
- Who they are and their situation
- What they were trying to get done, and the last time it happened
- How they do it today, and what that costs them in time, money or irritation
- What they have already tried or paid for
- Their own words — a few exact quotations
- Signals: asked to pay, to be kept informed, to introduce someone — or only polite interest
- What this changes in the hypothesis; follow-ups as todos
Record what they did and said, not what they predicted they would do.

### weekly-review
The measures against last week · what shipped or was finished · what was learned (from customers, from numbers) · what is stuck · the one most important thing for next week · todos with dates.

### decision-record
Context · options considered · the decision and who made it · why · what would make us revisit it · date to check the outcome (as a todo).

### experiment
Assumption being tested · the smallest test that could prove it wrong · what result counts as pass or fail, written before running · result · what happens next.

### stakeholder-update
A short monthly update in `outputs/` for investors, a board, a manager or advisers: headline · measures · progress against last month's goals · what went badly · goals for next month · specific asks. Drafted from the month's logs; the user edits before sending.

### launch-checklist
Who it is for and the one message · what must work on day one · pricing and how people buy · support and what to do when things break · how success is measured in the first two weeks · announcement drafts in `outputs/` · review date.

## Worked example
Project "Example — appointment reminders for small clinics": problem and hypothesis written out, two pieces of evidence (one for, one against), plan with now / next / later, riskiest assumption named; one customer-conversation meeting note; todos "Talk to five more clinic managers", "Price test with two options", "Decide build or stop" with dates.

## Care points
- Keep evidence and enthusiasm apart: label what is observed, what is inferred and what is hoped. Challenge plans that rest on untested assumptions.
- Customer names and what they said in confidence stay in their customer item; updates and public material in `outputs/` use them only with permission.
- Financial figures are recorded as the user gives them, with dates; never fill gaps with estimates presented as facts.
- Legal, tax and fundraising terms vary by place — flag what needs a professional's review.
