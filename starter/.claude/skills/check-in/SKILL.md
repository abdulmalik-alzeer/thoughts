# Check-in

A short recurring conversation that keeps the personal profile current and tracks progress. Two rhythms — infer which from the user's words and the date of the last entry in `me/log.md`; ask only if truly unclear. Speak and write in the user's language, in plain words.

## Daily (default) — about 5 minutes

1. Ask in **one batch**: What did you do today? What did you learn? Did anything change — plans, people, money, health?
2. Follow up on the single most consequential answer with one or two questions. A debrief, not an interrogation.
3. Glance at the todo lists: mention anything overdue or due tomorrow, and tick off what the user says is finished.

## Weekly — step back

1. First read the week's daily entries in `me/log.md`, `me/goals.md` if it exists, and the todos due in the coming week. Open with a three-line recap of the week as recorded, so the user reacts instead of recalling.
2. Ask: wins and misses against goals? What moved or stalled on active clients, projects or ideas? How were energy and habits? What is the one focus for next week?
3. Push back where the record disagrees with the self-assessment — that is the point of the meeting.

## Save

4. Add at the top of `me/log.md` (create it with a heading on first use; newest first; archive past about 400 lines):

   ```
   ## YYYY-MM-DD — daily | weekly review
   - Did: …
   - Learned: …
   - Changed: …
   - Next: …
   ```

   A distillation, not a transcript; leave out empty categories. No client-confidential details here — refer to work by item name only.

5. **Lasting facts** (a new skill, a changed situation, a revised goal) → the relevant `me/` file, not the log. If something big shifted and `me/interview-map.md` exists, reopen that domain there.
6. **Commitments** the user makes for tomorrow or next week → todos, with `@due(YYYY-MM-DD)` when a day was named.
7. **Spillover:** if the check-in shows real movement on an item, offer to update that item's log and front page too.
8. **Save** — outside the Thoughts app only: `me: check-in YYYY-MM-DD` (or `me: weekly review YYYY-MM-DD`).
